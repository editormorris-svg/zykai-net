Training & capability

AI adoption is a people programme as much as a technology programme.

ZYK’s training concept connects classroom practice, leadership decisions, technical operations and responsible use. The goal is not generic AI awareness: it is to help each group understand what they can do inside the institution’s own governed AI environment.

Role-based capability

Different people need different AI skills.

Teachers

Practical workflows for planning, differentiation, resource creation, feedback, verification and responsible student use.

Subject specialists

Department-level work with curriculum knowledge, rubrics, assessment design, prompt libraries and subject-specific evaluation.

School leadership

Strategy, governance, budgeting, adoption sequencing, risk ownership and meaningful measures of educational value.

IT & system administrators

Users, roles, model deployment, monitoring, backup, troubleshooting, integrations, security controls and escalation.

Students

AI literacy, verification, responsible creation, attribution, media awareness and project-based use within school policy.

Parents & families

Clear explanations of school AI use, data boundaries, oversight and practical ways to support responsible use at home.

Programme concepts

AI Fundamentals for Teachers

One-day practical introduction to classroom use, prompt design, lesson workflows, review habits, safeguarding and school policy.

Advanced AI for Subject Specialists

Two-day programme focused on curriculum design, subject workflows, rubrics, assessment and resource creation.

AI for School Leadership

Half-day strategic briefing on adoption, governance, budget planning, risk, staffing and evaluation.

ZYK System Administration

Three-day technical track covering platform operations, identity, monitoring, updates, troubleshooting and support procedures.

AI Model Management

Advanced technical work on model evaluation, local serving, quantisation, capacity planning and controlled model updates.

Cybersecurity for School AI

Access control, logging, incident response, data handling and governance evidence for IT and compliance teams.

Student AI Literacy

An eight-week concept from the business plan covering responsible use, prompt literacy, verification, media awareness and projects.

Summer AI Camp

A proposed one-week hands-on programme combining AI projects, infrastructure awareness, ethics and career exploration.

Parent AI Education

A proposed two-hour session explaining school AI, data/content controls and responsible family use.

Continuous capability

Training should continue after launch.

Models, school policies and classroom practice will change. ZYK’s longer-term training model therefore combines launch workshops with follow-up support, Q&A, leadership review and train-the-trainer capacity.
