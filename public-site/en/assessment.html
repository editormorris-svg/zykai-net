ZYK Assess

Assessment should be part of the institutional AI environment.

ZYK Assess is the proposed assessment layer for diagnostic testing, internal exams, mock exams, listening and speaking practice, AI-assisted feedback and progress analytics. It brings together ideas already present in the ZYK business plan with recovered Cambridge KET/PET assessment work.

Assessment lifecycle

From placement to progress.

Diagnostic & placement

Entrance, placement and baseline testing can help schools identify starting levels and appropriate learning pathways.

Practice & preparation

Question banks, mock exams and guided practice can support internal assessments and preparation for external exam formats.

Feedback

AI can assist with formative feedback, error patterns and next-step suggestions while leaving consequential academic judgement with qualified educators.

Progress analytics

Longitudinal reporting can help teachers and leaders see patterns across students, classes, subjects and terms.

Modalities

Assessment is more than multiple choice.

Reading & language use

Structured question formats, signs/notices, matching, gap-fill, comprehension and other browser-based item types.

Listening

Scripted listening tasks and controlled audio-generation workflows can support language practice and mock assessment.

Speaking

Future workflows can support pronunciation, structured practice, recording and teacher review. Formal scoring claims require validation.

Writing

Rubric-aware feedback and draft analysis can support teachers without presenting AI output as a substitute for professional marking.

Adaptive direction

Use difficulty intelligently — not opaquely.

The business plan includes adaptive proficiency testing. A responsible implementation would require a validated item bank, clear placement logic, appropriate psychometric review and teacher visibility into how recommendations are produced.

Adaptive pathways

Future tests could select items according to demonstrated performance rather than presenting every learner with the same fixed sequence.

Teacher-readable outputs

Results should translate into useful evidence: strengths, gaps, confidence ranges, recommended next steps and the items or skills behind the conclusion.

Institutional standards

Schools or groups could define approved testing windows, item pools, reporting formats and moderation processes.

External exam preparation

Practice formats without implying affiliation.

The ZYK source material includes IELTS, Cambridge KET/PET and TOEFL preparation concepts. These should be presented as independent practice/preparation services unless formal licensing or partnership exists.

Cambridge A2 Key / B1 Preliminary

Recovered adjacent work includes browser-based question formats, listening-script development and plans for speaking practice. This is useful product R&D, not evidence of Cambridge endorsement.

IELTS / TOEFL

Potential mock and practice workflows can use institution-owned or appropriately licensed content and ZYK-created practice materials.

School internal exams

The strongest initial use case may be institution-owned assessment, where schools control curriculum alignment, rubrics, item governance and reporting.

ZYK is not represented here as an official Cambridge, IELTS or TOEFL provider or partner. Any public use of third-party exam names, formats or materials requires appropriate rights and non-affiliation wording.

Why it belongs inside ZYK

Assessment becomes more useful when it shares identity, knowledge and governance.

Shared identity

Students, classes and teachers can use the same institutional identity and permissions layer.

Shared knowledge

Assessment can align to approved curriculum, rubrics and school learning objectives through ZYK Knowledge.

Shared control

Access, audit, retention, teacher review and reporting rules can sit within ZYK Control rather than in a disconnected assessment app.
